DriveCheckUA

Independent · App Store release

DriveCheckUA shows regional safety alerts on iPhone and CarPlay, with an on-device AI summary. Built with AI agents, from planning to App Store release. Directed the coding agents, reviewed their changes, and verified behavior with deterministic tests and device checks before release. Architected the client, on-device AI integration, and CarPlay UI. Swift code classifies the alert status, and the model writes the summary text from the facts it is given. The AI integration checks model availability, limits generation time, handles cancellation, validates output, and falls back deterministically. Also built home-screen and secondary-region widgets, a Control Center control, App Shortcuts, a Live Activity, and StoreKit 2 subscriptions with purchase verification and unit tests. All targets build in Swift 6 language mode with complete data-race checking. An earlier bounded agent/tool runtime, tested by a 38-case scripted evaluation corpus covering tool selection, execution budgets, malformed results, injection-like inputs, cancellation, and deadlines, was later removed from the app. Released on the App Store.

  • Integrated an on-device Foundation Models summary with validation and fallback
  • Added widgets, a Live Activity, and StoreKit 2 subscriptions in Swift 6 language mode

OneCart Family

Independent · App Store release

OneCart Family is a shopping list that a family shares through iCloud. Built and maintained with AI agents. Led the coding agents that built it, reviewed their changes, and investigated defects. Led architecture, data synchronization, and release verification. Core Data uses private and shared CloudKit stores with CKShare invitations. Edits are saved locally first, then synced to iCloud. The app handles membership changes, duplicate records, and recovery when cloud account deletion fails. WidgetKit snapshots and App Intents support widget actions. XCTest regression suites cover cart state, sharing, persistence, synchronization errors, and deletion recovery. Released on the App Store and still being developed.

  • Implemented Core Data and CloudKit persistence, CKShare invitations, and offline edits
  • Added widget actions and account-lifecycle flows with regression tests

Birmarket: marketplace and loyalty

PASHA Holding

Birmarket, called Umico until July 2025, is a PASHA-ecosystem marketplace with over 2 million customers. Its operator, Competo, has been a PASHA Holding subsidiary since March 2020. Worked on the Loyalty team alongside mobile, backend, QA, and product specialists while the iOS team grew from about five to twenty developers. Developed shopping and loyalty features, extracted shared modules, and later owned the multi-host Premium Subscription SDK on iOS. Company context · About Birmarket. Birmarket, the Birbank banking app, and the M10 payment wallet integrated the same remote Swift package. Each host handled authentication, session lifecycle, and token refresh, then passed a scoped access token through a small public API. The SDK managed its own networking. Proposed PostHog and designed the subscription SDK's own analytics layer, separate from the hosts' Firebase analytics. Built the slide-based subscription onboarding that replaced a long scrolling page. A/B tests cut it from five screens to three, which raised new-subscriber conversion. Created remote-configuration flags and handled them in code, such as the flag that switched between payment-gateway versions. Redesigned screens shipped behind kill switches that could return a single screen to its old design. Wrote unit and integration tests covering how subscription and loyalty benefits change prices, cart state, and final totals. Configured Bitrise build, test, and code-signing steps in the development, testing, and release workflows. Handed the SDK over while it was live in production in all three host apps.

  • Reimplemented Premium Subscription as a configurable multi-host SDK distributed as a Swift package
  • Defined host/SDK API boundaries with backend and other teams
  • Carried about seven of the app's 30+ feature modules through extraction, and broke cyclic dependencies by moving shared business logic, such as discount recalculation, into small modules behind protocols
  • Traced an abrupt crash to a retain cycle between a timer and a player delegate with the Memory Graph Debugger and the Leaks instrument, and removed scroll freezes by moving network calls off the main thread

Drinkit: coffee ordering app

Drinkit

Drinkit is an app-first coffee brand within Dodo Brands. Worked in a product team of about twenty people across mobile, backend, design, QA, product, and hardware. Brought an existing mobile team of three iOS and two Android engineers into Drinkit and built the iOS app with them. Shaped features with product management and chose the initial architecture together with the other iOS engineers. The app used MVP with coordinators, repository and service layers, and Swift Package Manager modules. Owned the dynamic menu, the app's signature feature: the backend held a deliberately rich catalog, and a client-side constructor composed each customer's menu from it by time of day and by preferences collected at first launch and after orders. Implemented its looping drink videos, offline caching of menu data and videos, and background downloads through a background URLSession. Drink customization relied on ingredient dependencies, option availability, and backend pricing parameters. Implemented much of that functionality, integrated backend-selected offers, and displayed order status through polling. Integrated a payment SDK built by another engineer. The app connected customer orders to backend preparation state and in-shop pickup displays. Worked with A/B tests, feature flags, and remote configuration, and took part in the business decisions made from A/B test results. Launched the app with the first coffee shop, and the chain grew to eight locations during two years of development.

  • Built and released the iOS app with two other iOS engineers for the first coffee-shop launch
  • Developed and refined drink customization as the product grew
  • Integrated contextual offers using shared client and backend data
  • Displayed order-preparation status through backend polling
